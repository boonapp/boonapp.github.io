Decisions

A prototype. No money moves, Face ID is a pretend scan, and checks are simulated: an account number shorter than 6 characters isn't found, one ending in 000 is already paid, and every profile link passes its ownership check. Screening lists are made-up samples: try Ravi Deshmukh for a public official, Viktor Sample for a sanctions match, or the city Sevastopol for a sanctioned region. Exchange rates are fixed samples. Detailed tax rules cover India, the US, the UK and the eurozone; other countries get general guidance and a US$2,000 cap per payment. Boon isn't offered under broad sanctions, such as in Iran, North Korea, Syria, Cuba, Russia, Belarus and occupied regions of Ukraine. Fees and rules are simplified summaries as of November 2026, not tax or legal advice. Real payments need a licensed partner in each country.
